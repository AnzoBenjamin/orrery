- [x] 2. Planar solver
  - [x] 2.1 Implement state layout, the `Constraint` trait, and revolute, prismatic, and driver constraints with analytic Jacobians.
    - _Requirements: 3.2, 4.1_
    - `kinematics::model` compiles a graph into `(x, y, θ)` per moving planar body (the ground fixed, a spatial ground projected into each plane), and `kinematics::constraint` implements revolute, prismatic, and angle and displacement drivers with analytic Jacobians, checked against finite differences by `proptest`. A prismatic joint holds B parallel to A (documented on the Zod schema). Joint kinds and spatial bodies beyond this build are refused with `InvalidGraph` naming the joint; 2.3 and 4.1 add them.
  - [x] 2.2 Implement Levenberg–Marquardt assembly with `faer` sparse and dense paths, seeded branch continuity, and `AssemblyFailed` diagnostics.
    - _Requirements: 4.1, 4.2, 4.3_
    - `kinematics::assembly`: Levenberg–Marquardt with Marquardt scaling, each step a damped least-squares QR (`faer` dense below 30 unknowns, sparse QR above, selectable for tests). A seed keeps the branch; without one, deterministic starting poses are built outward from the ground. Failures return `AssemblyFailed` with the three worst joints. Tested against closed-form four-bar and slider-crank positions through a full turn, with the branch held.
  - [x] 2.3 Implement pin-in-slot, gear mesh (external, internal, rack and pinion), belt, and cam-follower constraints, plus cam Fourier profiles and slot curves.
    - _Requirements: 3.1, 3.2, 3.5, 3.6_
    - Pin-in-slot and cam contact carry their curve parameter as an extra unknown; gears and belts relate angles to the carrier both wheels are pinned to; knife, roller, and flat-faced followers; slot curves (line, arc, cubic B-spline by de Boor) and Fourier cam profiles with derivatives and a tolerance-bounded polyline (`kinematics::curves`). A pin past its slot's end or a slider past its limits is an `AssemblyFailed`. The slots golden was redrawn so every slot holds its pin through a full turn.
  - [x] 2.4 Implement spring energy terms and constrained energy minimization.
    - _Requirements: 4.4_
    - `kinematics::energy`: projected Gauss–Newton in the SVD null space of the constraint Jacobian, re-assembling after each step with a backtracking line search. Tested against the closed-form and brute-force least-energy pose of a sprung lever.
  - [x] 2.5 Property tests 1, 2, and 9 with `proptest` mechanism generators.
    - _Requirements: 4.1, 3.2_
    - `tests/properties.rs` generates Grashof crank-rocker four-bars, offset slider-cranks, and Watt six-bars that turn a full revolution (property 1 at random angles without a seed, property 2 through a turn with continuation); property 9 over random gear ratios is in `tests/planar_joints.rs`.

- [x] 3. Simulation and analysis
  - [x] 3.1 Implement continuation with adaptive steps, reachable sub-ranges, and singularity detection and classification.
    - _Requirements: 5.1–5.4_
    - `kinematics::simulate`: continuation with a secant predictor, step halving on non-convergence or a body turning more than 0.3 rad, reachable sub-ranges with re-acquisition past a gap, and trajectories of every named point. Dead centers at range ends (full Jacobian singular, joints full rank), lockups where the output points' velocity reverses (located by bisection), and bifurcations where the joint rows lose rank (located by golden-section search). Tested on a crank-rocker (two lockups at the collinear positions), a rocker-driven four-bar (dead center at the swing limit), a non-Grashof crank (two reachable ranges), and a parallelogram (change point at π).
  - [x] 3.2 Implement velocities and accelerations with forward-mode dual numbers.
    - _Requirements: 5.5_
    - `kinematics::dual`: second-order Taylor numbers (`Jet`); every residual is written once over a `Scalar` trait. `kinematics::motion`: `J q′ = e_k`, `J q″ = −γ` with `γ` from one Jet pass, and `point_motion` for every named point. Tested against the closed-form slider-crank, finite differences on the goldens, and a property that the Jet's first-order term equals the analytic Jacobian product for every joint kind.
  - [x] 3.3 Implement Grübler and numerical mobility with redundant-constraint and free-motion reporting. Property test 4.
    - _Requirements: 6.1–6.3_
    - `kinematics::mobility`: Grübler–Kutzbach from unknowns minus joint equations (higher pairs counted once), numerical mobility from the SVD rank of the joint rows, redundant joints found by adding joints one at a time, and free motions from the null space of joints and drivers, given per body. Property 4 over generated linkages; a triple-crank parallelogram (Grübler 0, numerical 1, `top-3` redundant); free motions of an undriven four-bar and of the cam golden's unheld leaf.
  - [x] 3.4 Implement transmission angle, Grashof classification, swept collision detection with `parry2d`, and fabrication risk indicators.
    - _Requirements: 8.1–8.4_
    - `kinematics::plausibility`: four-bar loops found as revolute 4-cycles (frame, driven input, coupler, output), Grashof classes, least folded transmission angle at both floating joints, outline collisions with `parry2d-f64` (ear-clipped triangle compounds) between unjoined bodies in one plane or in parallel planes closer than their half thicknesses, located by subdividing each step and bisecting, slenderness from outlines, and quasi-static reaction reversals under springs and a unit load opposing each output point. Collisions are checked against a brute-force separating-axis test.
  - [x] 3.5 Property test 3 (continuity).
    - _Requirements: 4.2, 5.2_
    - `tests/properties.rs`: between samples with no singularity, every body coordinate moves at most twice its larger end speed times the step, over generated linkages and step counts. Writing it found that the six-bar generator only checked the dyad on one four-bar branch; it now requires both.

- [x] 4. Spatial support
  - [x] 4.1 Implement spatial body poses with quaternions and spherical, universal, cylindrical, and 3D-axis revolute and prismatic constraints.
    - _Requirements: 3.3_
    - `kinematics::spatial`: spatial bodies carry a position and quaternion with a unit-length row; joints are written in world coordinates against a `Placement` (fixed, planar in any plane, or spatial), with exact Jacobians from per-joint Jet passes. Spherical, universal, cylindrical, and 3D-axis revolute and prismatic (with limits), and drivers on spatial revolutes and sliders. Tested on a double Cardan shaft (constant velocity), the redrawn spatial RSSR, a new spatial slider-crank golden against its closed form, and a tilted slider, plus finite-difference Jacobians and point derivatives. When the drawn pose already satisfies the joints, assembly starts there.
  - [x] 4.2 Implement planar subsystems embedded in planes and connected by spatial joints, with `parry3d` collisions between subsystems.
    - _Requirements: 3.4, 8.3_
    - Planar bodies in any plane meet spatial joints through their `Placement` (the double Cardan golden joins two planar yokes). Outlines of bodies in crossing planes are extruded through their thickness into `parry3d-f64` prism compounds and tested in world poses; tested with two bars in perpendicular planes against a brute-force footprint check.
